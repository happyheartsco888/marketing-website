About Happy Hearts Co.

Short Version (1–2 sentences)

Happy Hearts Co. is South India's leading curator of flea markets, pop-up events, and brand exhibitions — championing women entrepreneurs, artisans, and home-grown businesses across Bangalore, Chennai, Coimbatore, and Mangalore. Founded by Vidhi Tanna, the company has produced 300+ events across malls, tech parks, and residential communities over a decade.

Long Version

Happy Hearts Co. was founded by hospitality veteran Vidhi Tanna with a single mission: to give South India's most talented small businesses — especially those led by women — a real platform to reach real audiences. Over a decade and 300+ events later, the company has become the region's most trusted name in curated market experiences.

Operating across Bangalore, Chennai, Coimbatore, and Mangalore, Happy Hearts Co. partners with leading malls, tech parks, and residential communities to produce flea markets, themed pop-ups, and brand exhibitions that drive measurable footfall, community engagement, and direct-to-consumer sales for independent vendors. The company has supported over 1,000 vendors — from home bakers and artisans to fashion designers and wellness entrepreneurs — in building brand visibility and sustainable businesses.
