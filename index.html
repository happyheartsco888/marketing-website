Our Story

Giving Small Brands the Stage They Deserve

Happy Hearts Co. was born from a belief that every handmade jar, home-baked treat, and crafted garment deserves a real audience. Founder Vidhi Tanna brings over a decade of hospitality expertise to every market she curates.

From bustling malls in Bangalore to residential communities in Coimbatore, we create spaces where shoppers discover genuine stories — and vendors find their tribe.
