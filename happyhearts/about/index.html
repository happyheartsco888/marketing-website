Meet the Founder

Vidhi Tanna

Vidhi Tanna has spent over a decade in hospitality — orchestrating experiences that people talk about long after the event is over. But it was the untold stories of South India's home-grown entrepreneurs that truly set her heart on fire.

She saw women baking extraordinary cakes from their kitchens with no platform to reach customers. She met artisans hand-crafting jewellery that rivalled luxury boutiques, selling quietly from Instagram DMs. She found home chefs with generations of recipes and nowhere to serve them.

So she built the stage. Happy Hearts Co. was born as a vehicle to give South India's most talented independent businesses the audiences they deserve — in the very spaces where people shop, work, and live.

The Journey

From One Market to 300+

2014

The First Event

Vidhi organises her first community market — a small weekend gathering of 15 home-based vendors in Bangalore. Every stall sells out.

2016

Happy Hearts Co. is Born

The brand is formalised. First mall partnerships signed in Bangalore. 40+ events in the first year under the Happy Hearts name.

2018

Expanding to Chennai

Happy Hearts Co. launches in Chennai with back-to-back sold-out markets at Express Avenue and VR Chennai.

2020

Pivoting & Growing Through Adversity

The pandemic year becomes a turning point — Vidhi supports vendors through digital pop-ups and emerges with a deeper community than ever.

2022

Coimbatore & Mangalore

Expansion into two new cities, bringing Happy Hearts Co.'s signature curation to new communities and new talent.

2025

300+ Events & Counting

A landmark milestone — and the beginning of the next chapter, with new city launches and a growing network of 1,000+ vendor alumni.
